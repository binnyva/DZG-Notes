This is a framework for a photographer to get their personal vision of the shot.

1. The first image is a documentary shot. This is what you just take if you want to record what's happening.
2. The what is the "best image" you can get in a technical sense. This depends on the device you are shooting at.
3. The third image is a unique vision that probably only you have in that situation.
